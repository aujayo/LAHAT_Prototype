Chatbot Assistant

A simple helper for three things: registration, clinic hours, and medicine problems. You can type in English or Filipino — small spelling mistakes are okay.

Kumusta po! I can help with registration, clinic hours, or a medicine problem. Press a button above, or just type.

How it works

This is a rule-based assistant, not an AI model. It matches your words to one of the three topics, and it tolerates typos, extra letters, and Tagalog/English mixes. For a medicine problem it asks what happened and which medicine, then sends a request using your saved section and address.

It does not give medical advice. For side effects or feeling unwell, it tells you to contact the health center.

Map Matrix

Ten sections plotted on your actual boundary. A section stays gray until a resident signs up or a report comes in — then it fills in with that section’s real residents, medicines, stock and requests, and changes as they change.
